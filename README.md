# Software Model for Improving Selectronic Company Using E-commerce Tools

## About the Project

This repository contains the research and software development project presented in the scientific article:

**"Software Model for Improving Selectronic Company Using E-commerce Tools"**

The study proposes a customized e-commerce platform for Selectronic, focused on improving inventory management, logistics, operational efficiency, and online sales processes.

The solution integrates e-commerce functionalities with internal inventory and logistics processes, while incorporating data analysis and visualization tools to support strategic business decision-making.

## Research Objective

The research evaluates the implementation of a customized e-commerce platform for Selectronic according to its inventory and logistics management requirements.

The proposed solution seeks to:

- Modernize Selectronic's digital presence.
- Improve inventory and stock management.
- Optimize logistics and supply chain coordination.
- Facilitate online transactions.
- Analyze business data for decision-making.
- Improve operational efficiency and market responsiveness.

---

## Methodology

The project followed an Agile software development approach.

The research incorporated:

- Field research.
- Bibliographic research.
- Observation.
- Interviews.
- User requirements analysis.
- User stories.
- Product backlog.
- Sprint-based development.
- System verification and evaluation.

A total of **298 participants** were involved in interviews to collect information related to user satisfaction and system requirements.

## Data Analysis

The system incorporates data analysis to support business decision-making.

The research uses the **ARIMA algorithm** to visualize and analyze best-selling products and support the interpretation of historical sales information.

The platform also provides graphical representations of:

- Best-selling products.
- Products with low stock levels.
- Sales distribution.
- Order status.
- Registered users.
- Geographic distribution of orders.
- Product categories.
- Business metrics.

---

## System Evaluation

The e-commerce application was evaluated through employee perception and usability analysis.

According to the study:

- **89.64%** of participants reported being satisfied or very satisfied with the overall application experience.
- **91.43%** expressed a positive tendency to recommend the application to other users.
- The study reported no participants who classified the overall experience as dissatisfied or very dissatisfied.

These results were obtained from the evaluation described in the research article.
